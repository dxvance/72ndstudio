About me

Hey, I’m David!

When I’m not working, you’ll find me on a climbing wall, at the piano, or halfway through a side project I definitely don’t have time for.

I run to clear my head. A sub-20 5K is the goal, and so far the 5K is winning. I’m relearning Mandarin one tone at a time, and I nerd out over Greek and Chinese mythology. Ask me about a hypothetical Marvel superhero matchup or how stock options work, and I’ll talk your ear off. I’m equally confident about both and am only right about one. If there’s a new Christopher Nolan movie out, I’m already in an IMAX seat on opening weekend.
